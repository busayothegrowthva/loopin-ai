Commitment Sync

When a client agrees to a date, a price or a deadline in WhatsApp, Loopin turns it into a real calendar event or task. You confirm with one tap.

Client: "Let's confirm the shoot for the 14th. I need the edited photos within two weeks."

Loopin creates: a calendar event on the 14th and a task called "Deliver edited photos" due two weeks later.

Revenue Sync

A price is agreed, so Loopin prepares the invoice and payment link. If it is unpaid after five days, it sends one polite reminder you approved.

Consistency Watch

Remembers what you promised across many chats. If you tell a client twice that you will send something and it still has not gone out, you get a private alert.

Accuracy Watch

Checks what a bot or team member told a customer against your real prices and policies. Wrong answers are flagged before more customers hear them.

Voice Sync

Send Loopin a voice note while you are busy. It becomes a tracked task with a due date, so nothing sits forgotten in your chat history.

Questions people ask.

Does Loopin replace Meta's WhatsApp AI?

No. Meta's AI talks to your customers. Loopin sits behind the conversation and makes sure what was agreed actually happens in your calendar, tasks and invoices. They work together.

Can Loopin read all my chats?

No. Loopin can only see a chat after you switch it on for that chat. Until then it does nothing. You can switch it off any time.

Will it send emails or book meetings without asking me?

No. Before it sends an email, books a meeting or creates an invoice, it shows you what it plans to do and waits for your Yes, Edit or No.

What if Loopin gets something wrong?

That is why you approve first. If it is unsure, it tells you instead of guessing. You can fix anything with one tap before it goes out.

Which tools does it connect to?

Your Google account (Gmail, Calendar, Tasks, Docs and Sheets) and WhatsApp. Payment links and invoicing tools are part of the Pro plan.

Does it work in my country?

Yes. Loopin is built for anyone who runs their business through WhatsApp, anywhere in the world.

Can I cancel?

Yes, at any time from your account. Cancel during the free trial and you are not charged.
